Set up in a minute

  1. Install on your Mac. Open the DMG and drag Mac n Droid to Applications. The first time, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  2. Install on your phone. Open the APK and allow installing from your browser or file manager.
  3. Pair. On the Mac, choose Pair a Phone. On the phone, tap Scan QR code.
  4. Allow access. Follow the Finish setup list on your phone. If notification access is greyed out, open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.
Everything stays on your Wi-Fi and is end-to-end encrypted. No account, no cloud, no ads.
