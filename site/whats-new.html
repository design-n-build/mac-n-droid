What's new

Every update, in plain words.

The latest version is 0.14.0. The Mac app updates itself; on Android, install the new version over the old one. All downloads are on GitHub Releases.

0.14.0Latest

Downloads
MacAndroidBrowser extensionWindows (beta)Linux (beta)
  • Pause sharing (Android): stay connected but share nothing for 1 hour, 3 hours, or until you resume. The switches you had on are turned off and come back exactly as they were. Pause from Home, the status notification, or three new Quick Settings tiles (Pause 1 hour, Pause 3 hours, Pause sharing). While paused, everything is dimmed and tapping it offers to resume. Find my phone and battery keep working. The Mac shows a "Sharing paused" bar. These tiles replace the connection tile.
  • Files from your phone, in the notch: a card with a preview, Open and Show in Finder.
  • Pairing codes work once: after your phone pairs, it gets its own secret key and the computer makes a new QR code, so a screenshot or copied pairing link of the old one is useless.
  • New phones need your OK: if a different phone scans the code, it waits until you allow it on the computer or on the phone that's already paired. Allowing asks for Touch ID or your Mac's password, or your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. Lost your phone? Just click Allow on the Mac.
  • Forward secrecy: every connection adds a one-time key exchange, so even a leaked key can't unlock traffic recorded earlier. Works with older versions too (they connect the old way until they update).
  • Safer file receiving: received files are marked as downloaded (macOS and Windows check apps and scripts before they first run), and names from the other device can't point outside Downloads.
  • New messages in an ongoing chat now show in the notch again; a run of messages in one chat alerts once, and again after 30 seconds of quiet. Progress-style updates (downloads) alert only the first time.
  • A new home: Mac n Droid now lives at github.com/design-n-build/mac-n-droid. Old links still work.

0.13.1

Downloads
MacAndroidBrowser extensionWindows (beta)Linux (beta)
  • A welcome on the Mac: the first time you open Mac n Droid, your phone and Mac appear linked by a live electric current, with the app's features circling them. Then a full-window pairing screen, with rings pulsing around the QR code while it waits.
  • Celebrate pairing, on both devices at once: the phone and the computer come together, a ✓ pops in, and confetti bursts across the Mac's screen and the phone. Play Confetti to do it again.
  • Pair a Phone, once paired: shows the phone you're paired with, and Pair a Different Phone when you need the code again. Mac n Droid works with one phone at a time.
  • Android: setup cards turn into a ✓ when you allow something, steps slide like pages, and the progress bar fills smoothly. Back swipes preview the previous screen.
  • Android: a gentle animation when the app opens; sent files lift off towards the computer; "Sent to …" confirms the clipboard; a glow traces the screen edge when the computer starts showing your screen.
  • Android: live progress in the notification while files move, and a Quick Settings tile that shows the connection and opens the app.
  • A friendlier Mac installer: the download window shows where to drag the app, how your phone and Mac connect, and what to do if macOS won't open it the first time.
  • Windows and Linux (beta): Linux downloads are named with the version, and the bug report form now asks for any computer, not only a Mac. There may still be a few bugs; please report them.

0.13.0

Downloads
MacAndroidBrowser extensionWindows (beta)Linux (beta)
  • Photos on the Mac: a new Photos page shows your phone's photos and videos, newest first, by album. Click one to open it in Quick Look, or save originals to Downloads. Nothing is copied until you open or save it. Turn it off any time with Photos on Mac on the phone's Home tab.
  • New controls under the mirrored screen: Back, Home, Recent, Type and Stop in one bar below the phone, never on top of it.
  • Android 17: if Android blocks Mac n Droid from your Wi-Fi network, the app now says so and asks for local network access, instead of searching forever.
  • Mac n Droid for Windows and Linux (beta), with the same features as the Mac app: notifications with Reply and call buttons, Messages (read and send texts), Phone screen (see and use your phone, with mouse and keyboard), music both ways, clipboard with history, files and folders both ways, links both ways, ring your phone, the browser extension, and the left-behind alert. Windows 10 and 11 (Intel, AMD and ARM); Linux as a Flatpak (x86_64 and ARM) or from source. The webcam works too: it's Android's own USB webcam.
  • Android says "PC" when paired with Windows or Linux, everywhere it used to say "Mac".
  • Volume, both ways: a slider for your phone's volume next to its music on the Mac's Overview and in the notch. On the phone, "Music on your Mac" works like a Cast device: a Mac volume slider in Android's media controls, volume buttons in its notification, and the phone's volume keys change the Mac's volume while that's what's playing.
  • The notch shows over every app again (with Stage Manager on, it could show only while Mac n Droid's own window was up).
  • Several live activities at once in the notch: music beside the notch with a timer or delivery running as small bubbles next to it. Click the notch to see them all, and click one to put it beside the notch. The phone's volume has a mute button (click the speaker).
  • Live activities end on the Mac as soon as you leave that app on the phone or lock it (a finished timer could stay up counting).
  • Browser extension: video calls (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams and others) no longer show up as music on your phone. Separate packages for the Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons.
  • Android: one setting instead of two. Clipboard sync and phone control are now a single optional accessibility setting, Mac n Droid clipboard and controls. It covers automatic copying, using the phone from your computer, links that open by themselves and music started from your computer. If you had clipboard sync on, it stays on; if you only had phone control on, turn on the new setting once (the phone reminds you).
  • Android: new first-run setup. A welcome screen, then Make it yours: turn on only the features you want before pairing, each with a plain explanation. Before Android's Accessibility settings open, the app explains exactly what the setting looks at and asks you to agree.
  • Android: setup checklist on the Home tab shows what's on and what's off, with an Enable button for each. If Android switches clipboard and controls off (it can happen after an update), the phone tells you.
  • Android: a link from your computer that arrives as a notification explains how to make links open by themselves.

0.12.1

Downloads
MacAndroidBrowser extension
  • Send folders, both ways. On the Mac, drag a folder onto Mac n Droid or the notch, pick one with Choose Files…, or use Share → Send to Phone in Finder. On the phone, tap Send files → Folder. The folder arrives with its subfolders, in Downloads (on the phone, Downloads › Mac n Droid).
  • Left-behind alerts explain how they work: they come when your phone leaves the Wi-Fi network your Mac is on (or your Mac joins another network without your phone), not at a set distance.
  • New browser extension icon: the white Mac n Droid logo. In light mode the toolbar shows a dark one.
  • Support email: help.macndroid@gmail.com.

0.12.0

Downloads
MacAndroidBrowser extension
  • Add the browser extension from the Mac app: click Browser Extension in the sidebar (or Settings → Sync → Browser → Add to Browser…). It explains what the extension does, then opens your browser's extension store: Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons or Firefox Add-ons. The Overview suggests it until an extension is connected.
  • Browser extension for Firefox.
  • Pairing code opens Google Play: scanning the Mac's pairing code with the phone's camera opens Mac n Droid on Google Play if it isn't installed. The pairing details stay on the phone.
  • The menu bar no longer has a Send Tab button. Press ⌃⌥P, or use the browser extension, to open the current page on your phone.
  • The Android app now targets Android 16.

0.11.1

Downloads
  • Browser extension: no more "connection refused" entries on the browser's Errors page when Mac n Droid isn't running. It now connects only when something plays, when you open its popup or use its menu, and waits longer between retries. The right-click menu shows ✓ or ! on the toolbar icon to say whether it worked.

0.11.0

Downloads
MacAndroid
  • Messages on your Mac: read and reply to texts from the Mac's new Messages page. Start a new conversation with contact search. Google Messages chat (RCS) messages that arrive while you're connected show up too, and replies go back through the chat.
  • Clipboard history: the last 20 things copied on either device, on both devices. Tap to copy again, or clear the list from the phone or the Mac.
  • Link handoff: share a link on the phone with Open on Mac to open it in the Mac's browser. On the Mac, press ⌃⌥P to open the current browser tab on the phone. If the phone is locked, the link opens when you unlock it.
  • Send to Phone in the Mac's Share menu: in Finder, Photos, Safari, Preview and any app with a Share button. Files and photos go to the phone, links open on it, and text goes to its clipboard.
  • Browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chrome-based browsers: pause, play and skip music and videos playing in the browser from your phone, and open pages and links on your phone.
  • Control your Mac's music from the phone: Music, Spotify and browser tabs appear in the phone's media controls.
  • Drag files onto the MacBook notch to send them to the phone.
  • Send files to Mac Quick Settings tile on the phone.
  • Automatic updates for the Mac app.
  • Left-behind alerts: a chime on the phone if you walk away from your Mac while it's awake.
  • Use your phone from the Mac while mirroring (needs the optional phone control setting).
  • The phone's share sheet shows two clear actions, Open on Mac and Send to Mac, with a laptop icon.
  • A burst of notifications (like WhatsApp re-posting old unread chats) plays one sound on the Mac and shows only the first few.
  • Calls disappear from the Mac as soon as they end, even when the phone is locked.
  • Clicking Reply in the notch puts the cursor straight in the text box. Replies that can only happen on the phone are labelled "Reply on Phone"; messages that can't be replied to have no Reply button.
  • Several files at once give one sound and one notification.
  • Android home screen: grouped settings in the style of Google's own apps, colors that follow your phone's theme, and the bottom bar in the order Home, Files, Clipboard, Notifications.
  • Softer brand colors and clearer app and extension icons.
  • The Mac app isn't notarized yet: the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  • To install the browser extension, open Mac n Droid → Settings → Sync → Browser → Show Extension in Finder, then load it in your browser's extensions page with Developer mode on.
  • Messages needs SMS access on the phone. Older Google Messages chats (RCS) stay on the phone; only new ones show on the Mac.
