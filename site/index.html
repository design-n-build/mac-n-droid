iPhone has Continuity. Windows has Phone Link. Android and Mac had nothing.

If you use an Android phone with a Mac, you know the routine: emailing photos to yourself, picking up the phone for every notification, typing codes by hand.

Mac n Droid connects the two the way Apple connects its own devices. Install it on both, scan one QR code, and your phone and your Mac work together from then on. It's built natively for each: a real Mac app, and a real Android app.

How to connect your Android phone to your Mac

Three steps, once. After that, they find each other on their own.

  1. 01

    Install Mac n Droid on your Mac

    Open the download and drag the app into Applications. Needs macOS 14 or later.

    Mac n Droid Applications
    Download for Mac Mac install help
  2. 02

    Install it on your Android phone

    Any brand with Android 10 or later. Open it and tap Get started.

    Mac n Droid
    Download for Android Android install help
  3. 03

    Scan the code on your Mac

    The Mac shows a QR code. On the phone, tap Scan QR code and point it at the screen.

    Keep both on the same Wi‑Fi
Done

That's it. They're connected, for good.

You pair once. From then on your phone and your Mac find each other by themselves, after sleep, restarts or a change of Wi‑Fi. Turn on only the features you want from the checklist on your phone.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Email help.macndroid@gmail.com or open an issue.

How do I connect my Android phone to my Mac?

Install Mac n Droid on your Mac and on your Android phone. On the Mac, click Pair a Phone; on the phone, tap Scan QR code and scan the code. Keep both on the same Wi‑Fi. That's it: they reconnect by themselves after that, even after a restart.

Can I get Android notifications on my Mac?

Yes. Your phone's notifications appear on your Mac as native banners, or drop down from the notch on a MacBook. You can reply, mark as read, and answer or decline calls. Dismiss a notification on the Mac and it's dismissed on the phone too.

How do I transfer files from Android to Mac without a cable?

On the phone, share any file or photo and choose Send to Mac, or use the Send files button or the Quick Settings tile. It lands in your Mac's Downloads folder over Wi‑Fi. To send the other way, drag files or folders onto the Mac n Droid window or the notch, or use Share → Send to Phone in Finder and other apps.

Can I text from my Mac with an Android phone?

Yes. You can read and reply to your text messages (SMS) from your Mac and start new conversations with contact search. Your phone still sends them, from your own number.

Can I copy and paste between Android and Mac?

Yes. Copy text or an image on one and paste it on the other. The last 20 things you copied are kept on both devices, so you can copy them again. Passwords from password managers are never sent.

Is there an AirDrop for Android and Mac?

AirDrop only works between Apple devices. Mac n Droid gives you the same idea between Android and a Mac: send files, photos, whole folders and links in a couple of taps, directly over your own Wi‑Fi.

Is there a Phone Link app for Mac?

Microsoft Phone Link only runs on Windows. Mac n Droid brings the same kind of connection to the Mac: notifications, texts, calls, files, clipboard, music and screen mirroring, between your Android phone and your Mac.

Is Mac n Droid free?

Yes, completely. There are no ads, no subscription and no account. Mac n Droid is open source under the GNU GPL v3.0 license, and all of its code is on GitHub.

Is it private? Does my data go through the cloud?

No cloud is involved. Your phone and your Mac talk directly to each other on your Wi‑Fi, encrypted end to end with AES‑256‑GCM using a key created when you scan the QR code. Mac n Droid has no servers and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.

Do my phone and Mac need to be on the same Wi‑Fi?

Yes. Mac n Droid connects the two directly over your local network, which is why nothing has to go over the internet. Leave the Wi‑Fi and it can tell you, so you don't leave your phone behind.

Which Android phones work with Mac n Droid?

Any phone with Android 10 or later, including Google Pixel, Samsung Galaxy, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Oppo, Motorola and Nothing. On some brands, set the app's battery use to Unrestricted so it stays connected.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple silicon or an Intel processor. On MacBooks with a notch, notifications and live activities can appear right at the notch.

Why does my Mac say Mac n Droid can't be opened?

The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once. More in the install help.

Can I mirror my Android screen on my Mac?

Yes. See your phone's screen in a window on your Mac, and with the optional clipboard and controls setting, click, scroll and type to use the phone from your Mac. You can also use your phone as a webcam over USB on Android 14 or later.

Does Mac n Droid work on Windows or Linux?

Yes, in beta. There's a Windows app for Windows 10 and 11, and a Linux app for GNOME and other desktops, with the same features and the same Android app. Download them here.
